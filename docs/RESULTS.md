# Results

This document lists what the simulations in this repository measured. Every number is copied from a
file in `results/` (git-ignored, regenerated by the commands given) or from the stdout of a command
that was re-run when this document was written. Each table or number block names its source file,
the command that produced it and, where a missed / ghost / RMSE-of-matched metric appears, the
match distance. Nothing here was measured on real sensor data.

## 0. How to read this

### Metrics

| Term | Definition (as printed in the scripts' legends and module docstrings) |
|---|---|
| RMSE | Position error of matched confirmed tracks, over matched (target, step) pairs only. A row that loses its tracks can show a lower RMSE; read it with missed. |
| missed | Share of in-view (target, step) pairs without a matched confirmed track. |
| window missed | The same share restricted to a focus window (the steps from the onset of a maneuver to the end of the run, or the outage windows in section 4). |
| ghost / step | Confirmed tracks with no target within the match distance, per step. It includes real tracks pulled off their target. |
| false / step | Confirmed tracks never matched to any target in the run (clutter-born). |
| ID switch | Identity changes of the track matched to a target, per run, counted after the burn-in. |
| NEES | Normalized estimation error squared, `e^T P^-1 e`. For a consistent filter its mean equals the state dimension: 4 for the state, 2 for position, 2 for velocity. |
| cross-range rms | Position error component perpendicular to the line of sight from the radar (section 5). |
| match distance | A track is matched to a target if it lies within this distance. 50 m for all scores of sections 3 (the compare run is given at 150 m and at 50 m), 4, 5 and the 50 m columns of 6 and 7; 200 m for the error diagnostics (cross-range, NEES, camera accept rates, and the cross-range columns of Table C); 150 m in the multi-target compare run of section 3. Each table has one match distance, printed next to it, with one exception: Table C in section 7 has RMSE columns at 50 m and cross-range columns at 200 m (the source file names the 200 m for the cross-range columns; the 50 m of the RMSE columns is explained in section 7). |

### Statistics

- Every experiment runs the same seeds at every row of a sweep (common random numbers), so truth,
  noise, detections and clutter are identical across rows.
- Cells are `mean +- half-width` of the 95% Student t interval over seeds. "d" is a paired
  difference, row (or arm) minus the neutral row (or the baseline arm), computed per seed, then
  summarized the same way.
- Seed sets: tuning seeds 1000-1019 (choose parameters), evaluation seeds 0-49 (used after the
  parameters were frozen), test seeds from 2000, pilots with 5 seeds (a does-it-run check; no
  conclusion is drawn from them).
- Pre-registration (section 6): the success criteria are code in `src/fusion/improvement_criteria.py`
  and the tuned parameters were frozen in commit b6a319f at 3354afc, before an evaluation seed was
  used. The high-Q control arms 3 and 5 and the reporting rule against them were added later, in
  16185d2; they are descriptive and not tuned.
- Criterion vocabulary (docstring of `improvement_criteria.py`): "improves" means the 95% interval
  of the paired difference excludes zero in that direction and the mean difference is at least the
  practical threshold (missed rate: max(0.01, 25% of the baseline's window degradation); RMSE and
  cross-range: max(1.0 m, 25% of the baseline's degradation); ghost rate 0.02 per step; ID switches
  0.5 per run). Neutral margins (no maneuver, no bias): RMSE 0.3 m, missed 0.005, ghost 0.01 per
  step, ID switches 0.2 per run.

## 4. Sensor outages

A radar, camera or total outage silences a sensor between 20 s and 20 s + duration (it delivers
neither detections nor clutter). The "unaware" tracker treats the lost scans as misses. The "aware"
tracker is told the radar is down: lifecycle counters are frozen, no track is born, tentative tracks
are dropped, and tracks coast until `max_coast_time` (15 s here unless stated) passes without any
update. Clean scene: no clutter, Pd 0.9, 50 seeds, 60 s. **Match distance: 50 m** for every table
of this section (it is printed in the file header). Windows: before = after the burn-in up to the
outage start; during = the outage; after = 30 s after the outage, clipped at the end of the run.

## 8. General limitations

- Simulation only. All sensors, targets and disturbances are generated by `src/fusion`; no real data
  was used, so the numbers describe the simulator and the tracker's beliefs about it.
- Planar (2D) geometry, one radar and one camera at or near the origin; a single constant-velocity
  baseline filter (the "EKF"), which is also the filter the maneuver rows disturb.
- The frozen parameters of the improvements were chosen by the fallback rule: no candidate met the
  neutral margins on the 20 tuning seeds (section 6.1). Ties were broken toward the smaller value.
- The ID-switch margin is flagged WIDER in the tuning output (section 6.1) and the criteria file
  lists UNDERPOWERED checks (section 6.3); the intervals are not adjusted for the many comparisons
  ("family of 108 checks, unadjusted").
- The camera-bias estimate leaves a residual error at 1 deg bias (Table C, section 7).
- The 5-seed pilot runs in `results/pilot*` are not evidence.
- The "two close targets both deleted" limitation was not triggered in the scenes measured
  (section 4.3); no statement is made about other scenes.
- The robustness tables of section 5 are single factors changed one at a time; combined disturbances
  are only in the "hard" group of section 6 (`results/improvement_hard.txt`), not reproduced here.
- Sections 1 and 2 use their own single-target scenes ("near", "far") and have no association step.
